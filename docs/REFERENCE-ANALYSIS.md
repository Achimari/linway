# Reference analysis

Reviewed 1 October 2026 from the live homepages of [Postevand](https://postevand.com) and [Lóvi](https://lovi.care), using rendered desktop (1440 × 1000) and mobile (390 × 844) captures. The captures in `docs/references/` are internal research only and are excluded from Git/deployment. Both sites can change after this date.

The references point to a shared quality: confidence through scale, space, and a small number of clear ideas. linway should borrow those principles while creating a quieter, more personal identity suited to reading and supporter updates.

## Postevand

### Observed

- The header is a strict, thin-lined grid: wordmark, a few navigation choices, commerce actions. It establishes structure before the image starts.
- A full-bleed sky photograph dominates the opening screen. The subject is positioned off-centre and the brand name sits at extreme scale near the lower edge.
- Most of the palette comes from the photograph; interface elements remain nearly monochrome.
- Interior sections use very large type, hairline rules, asymmetric columns, oversized product imagery, and generous blank areas.
- On mobile, the navigation compresses to wordmark, one action, and a menu; the hero crop carries the experience.
- The capture revealed an immediate newsletter modal at both sizes. It obscured the hero and later content, demonstrating why linway should not interrupt a first visit with signup UI.

### Useful for linway

- Let `MainPage.jpg` provide the opening atmosphere and blue palette.
- Use a slim rule-based header and one confident wordmark rather than a decorative church logo.
- Keep one memorable large-type moment and strong left/right composition.
- Use rules to connect narrative moments, giving the journey a sense of continuation.

### Do not copy

- Giant wordmark over the hero would compete with Lina’s name and story.
- Product-commerce navigation, constant uppercase microtype, and hard retail calls to action do not fit a ministry journal.
- A full-screen signup popup would feel transactional and adds privacy/integration work before the site has earned it.
- The exact photographic composition, branding, typography, and section layouts belong to Postevand.

## Lóvi

### Observed

- The hero uses a compact logo, centred headline, one short supporting line, one primary store action, and a large demonstration image.
- The headline is large but its line length is tightly controlled. The mobile version preserves the hierarchy and recentres every element rather than squeezing a desktop row.
- Later sections pair large explanatory type with close-up product/interface visuals and abundant white space.
- Repetition is used as a product narrative: named “superpowers,” demonstrations, evidence, then social proof.
- Rounded phone surfaces and small luminous accents belong to the product concept; most of the page remains black, white, and image-led.
- A cookie control is visible in the desktop captures. It is functional website infrastructure, not a visual device to reproduce unless linway actually uses consent-requiring tools.

### Useful for linway

- Keep the hero message to one strong thought and one supportive sentence.
- On mobile, deliberately reorder and recrop instead of relying on the desktop composition.
- Give each section one job: introduce, tell the journey, show updates, invite support.
- Build a consistent narrative system such as `Learn · Serve · Grow · Discern` only where Lina confirms it reflects the programme.

### Do not copy

- Phone mockups, floating badges, glowing marks, and product-feature demonstrations would turn a personal journal into an app landing page.
- Claims, awards, metrics, testimonials, and endorsement patterns need real evidence and are unnecessary here.
- Centring every section would weaken long-form readability. linway’s journal should use a stable reading column.

## Translation into linway

| Reference principle | linway interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| One dominant hero image | Supplied Bible-and-sky image, with text in its genuine negative space and a safe mobile crop |
| Confident, tightly wrapped headline | “Faith, people, and the next step.” in a restrained editorial serif |
| Minimal structural navigation | `linway` wordmark plus My story, Journal, Support |
| Strong section rhythm | Wide atmospheric hero followed by warm paper sections and thin horizon-like rules |
| Feature chapters | Human journey moments: faith, service, a new country, current chapter |
| High visual repetition | Repeated date/section markers and a subtle path line; no stacked cards |
| Mobile redesign | Text first, image second; full-width actions only when helpful; no cropped-off Bible |

## Original design position

The memorable device should be a **horizon line**: a fine blue rule that moves through the page, becomes the baseline for journey markers, and separates journal entries. It connects the sky photograph to the name “linway” without becoming a literal road graphic.

Use soft sky blue, ink, warm paper, and one muted oxblood accent picked up from the Bible ribbon/nails. The result should feel like a personal field journal with daylight around it—more intimate than either reference and clearly built for reading.

No visual asset, copy, logo, or screenshot from either reference should ship with linway. They are inspiration sources, not templates.
