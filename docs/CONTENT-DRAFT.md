## Story page — ready to refine

### A journey of faith

My name is Lina. I grew up in Omsk in a Christian family, with parents who had moved there as missionaries from Latvia. Church life and people serving God were part of my world from the beginning.

For a long time, faith was part of the environment around me. During my teenage years, it became personal. I began to understand God’s love for myself, chose to give my life to Christ, and was baptised at 17.

Serving became an important part of that journey. I spent time in teenage and youth ministry, helped at children’s, family, and youth camps, joined worship teams, and worked on church events and creative projects. Some responsibilities were practical: welcoming people, organising a team, keeping track of details, or preparing music. The deeper work was relational—learning to listen, build trust, and care for people consistently.

In 2023, I moved to Latvia to study Business and Management. Starting again in another country meant a new language environment, new relationships, studies, work, and the ordinary challenge of building a life somewhere unfamiliar. That season taught me flexibility and humility. It also showed me that the heart of service stays the same across places: to be faithful where God has put me and to care for the people in front of me.

As I approached the end of my studies, I began asking what might come next. Further study and professional work were both possible, but I wanted time to grow in faith, understand ministry more deeply, and discern my direction without rushing.

I then learned about the Ministry Training Scheme at All Souls in London and was accepted for its 2026–27 programme. What drew me to it was the combination of church life, biblical and theological learning, practical service, mentoring, and relationships. Cross-cultural ministry especially matters to me because I know what it feels like to live and learn in a language that is not your first. A patient, welcoming conversation can create space for questions that are difficult to ask elsewhere.

I do not know every step ahead. I want to keep learning, serve faithfully, and pay attention to where God may be leading. Linway is where I will share that journey as it unfolds.
